# Brisk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Brisk does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- **No network access.** Brisk makes no network requests and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code.
- **Local only.** System figures (CPU, memory, disk, battery) are read on your Mac and shown in the app. They never leave your device.
- **Folders you choose.** The Mac App Store version reads a folder only after you pick it in the system folder dialog. File names, sizes and checksums used to find large files and duplicates stay in memory and are discarded when you close the app.
- **No accounts.** Brisk has no sign-in and no user profile.
